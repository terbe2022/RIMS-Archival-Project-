CONFIDENTIAL REVIEW - Manuscript ECOAPP-2021-1172 (synthetic)
Title: 'Urban vacant lots as refugia for cavity-nesting bees'

Summary: The authors survey 40 vacant lots in a midwestern city with trap nests and claim lots older than 15 years support more cavity-nesting species.

Major concerns:
1. Lot age is confounded with distance to the river corridor; please include distance as a covariate.
2. Trap-nest occupancy was scored once, in October, so early-season species are missed.
3. The unpublished data from the Gregson lab cited in Table 2 cannot be evaluated.

Recommendation: major revision.
Reviewer: M. Achterberg
