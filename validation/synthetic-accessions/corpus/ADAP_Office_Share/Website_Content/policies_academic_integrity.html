Academic Integrity

This policy describes how the College handles suspected academic integrity violations in undergraduate courses.

An instructor who suspects a violation meets with the student, documents the evidence, and may assign a sanction up to failure of the course.

A second documented violation in any CAES course is referred automatically to the College Academic Integrity Board, which may recommend suspension (the 'two-strike' rule).

Instructors must report every finding to the Associate Dean's office within 5 working days so that repeat violations can be identified.

Students may appeal to the Associate Dean within 10 working days.
