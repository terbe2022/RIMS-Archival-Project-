Revised 12 August 2026. The University Archives answered the outstanding scoping questions, and several answers changed the direction of this work rather than filling in blanks. Material does not arrive on drives we have to attach — it is already on a network share, preservation-processed, with 49 collections waiting for appraisal review. Scale is 100–10,000 files per accession, not millions. Delivery runs to Medusa, the Library Digital Library and ArchivesSpace. Stages 00, 02, 05, 06, 07 and the software and governance sections are updated below; the full record is in docs/stakeholders/answers-2026-08.md.

A drive arrives. It belonged to a researcher who has retired or died. Somewhere in its hundreds of thousands of files is work worth keeping — and no practical way to find it, because appraising it by hand means opening all of it.

This document is the working plan for the system that answers one question at scale: which of these files matter? Everything expensive happens only after that question is answered. Each stage below lists what has to be true before the next one can start, what we can get from software that already exists, what we still have to build, and who is holding it.
