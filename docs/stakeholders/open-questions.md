# Institutional decisions requiring evidence

Institutional owners retain authority over appraisal policy, infrastructure, protected storage and release. Existing answers and implemented fields must be read with their dates and scope; neither automatically resolves a new operational approval.

Decision areas requiring current scope and operational acceptance evidence include ratified appraisal rules and the implementation of historically approved human oversight defaults, approved evaluation references, destination metadata contracts, protected storage and pseudonymization custody, working-copy authentication, retention/disposal authority, and donor or access restrictions. Confirm the decision and responsible authority from evidence rather than assigning a date or owner by inference.

Use the [decision register](../DECISIONS.md), [draft appraisal rules](../design/appraisal-rules-draft.md) and [dated August answers](answers-2026-08.md). Actionable follow-up belongs in existing GitHub issues and Project fields. The [earlier question list](../history/docs/stakeholders/open-questions.md) is preserved as historical context.
