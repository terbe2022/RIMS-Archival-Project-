Identity

Folder prefixes are record-series numbers, assigned by archivists. They are not unique delivery identifiers. Unnumbered formal accessions are set aside.

Research vocabulary and image context

Standard vocabularies plus archivist-defined local terms are a proposed addition to accession context. Relevant research terms can support grounded tagging and review prioritization, without turning a keyword match into an automatic retention decision. Keep descriptions faithful to the file and preserve unexpected evidence.

People and event tags need captions, documented context or archivist confirmation. Do not name a person from a face alone or assume an unidentified gathering’s significance. These are design proposals, not completed features.

Human review, approved storage and source-based evaluation remain necessary. No original accession content, private service endpoint or credentials are included here.
