# Appraisal policy references

The [historical appraisal rubric](history/docs/appraisal-rubric-v0.1.md) is supporting policy rationale, not a newly ratified operating rule. Read [draft appraisal rules](design/appraisal-rules-draft.md) and [decisions](DECISIONS.md) for current authority and unresolved choices.

Appraisal policy and model-description grading serve different purposes. Sensitivity takes priority; no model score authorizes autonomous disposal or release. Use [validation grading](../validation/GRADING.md) for experiment assessment and institutional approval for policy.
